The problem

Packages are now a full-time job

Staff time

Every box signed for, logged, shelved, and handed back is time your team isn't spending on leasing, renewals, or residents.

Theft & "where's my package?"

Packages left at doors and breezeways go missing, and the complaints, emails, and bad reviews land on the office.

The package room

Overflowing shelves, after-hours pickups, and a room that was never meant to hold a holiday season's worth of boxes.

Liability

Once staff accept a package, the property is involved if it's damaged or lost. A locker takes the office out of that chain.

How it works

Three people, zero handoffs

The courier

Scans in and drops the package into a right-size door. The door locks, and the drop is logged with a time stamp.

The resident

Gets a pickup code instantly and collects the package 24/7, with no office hours and no waiting.

Your team

Nothing to sign or shelve. If a resident asks where a package is, the log shows when it arrived and when it was picked up.

Common Questions

Who pays for the lockers?+

That's your decision. Some communities budget it as a capital amenity, and others recover the cost through a resident amenity or package fee. Our quote breaks out every cost so you can model either approach.

Do all carriers use it?+

The system is built for courier drop-off with a scan-in workflow. During setup we onboard the carriers that deliver to your property and give your staff a short guide for exceptions.

What about oversized packages?+

We size the door mix to your actual deliveries, including large compartments. Anything that doesn't fit goes to the office as it does today, just far less often.

How much space and power do we need?+

It depends on the model. Mains-powered Prime needs an outlet and a network connection, while solar Flex and battery Lite don't. Your quote lists the exact requirements.

How fast can it be installed?+

Lead time depends on the configuration and order. Your quote includes an estimated date for each stage, from order to first delivery.
