About Zen Lockers

A locker company started from the driver's seat

Zen Lockers is a Houston company that quotes, installs, and services smart parcel and food-pickup lockers for apartment communities, restaurants, and workplaces. We were founded in 2025 by a working delivery driver who was tired of watching packages disappear from doorsteps, and we've kept the business deliberately simple: commercial-grade hardware from certified manufacturers, installed and supported by the people you actually talk to.

Why We Exist

Our founder delivers packages for a national carrier. Every shift he sees the same thing: a parcel left in the open, a delivery photo taken, and thirty seconds later nothing on the step. He has been met at the curb by people asking for packages that weren't theirs. The "delivered" photo is the only record anyone has of what happened.

A smart locker fixes the problem at the exact moment it happens. The courier opens a door, sets the package inside, and the door locks. The resident gets a code. Nobody else can open it, and every open is logged with a time and an ID. It is a small piece of hardware that removes an entire category of complaint from a property manager's week.

We started Zen Lockers to put that hardware into Houston buildings — and to be the local company that answers when a door sticks or a screen goes dark, instead of a national call center.

What We Do — and Don't

Plain about our role

We quote and design

Site walk, door-mix recommendation, placement drawing, power and network check, and a fixed price with hardware, installation, and software itemized.

We install and configure

Delivery, assembly, anchoring, software setup, carrier and resident onboarding. We stay until the first packages go in.

We service locally

Support from Houston. If something fails, you call the person who installed it and they come out.

We don't manufacture

Our lockers come from ISO 9001-certified manufacturers with UL/ETL-listed and NSF-certified product lines. Their certifications are theirs; the install and the service are ours.

Where we are today

Zen Lockers is an early-stage company. We are actively quoting and scheduling our first Houston installations for 2026, and we are open to conversations with property owners, operators, and investors who want a local locker partner rather than a national vendor. If you'd like references, specifications, or vendor documents (W-9, certificate of insurance), ask — we'll send what we have and tell you plainly what we don't yet have.
