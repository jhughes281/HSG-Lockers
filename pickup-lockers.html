Restaurant Pickup Lockers & Pods

Orders wait warm and secure behind a locked door; guests and delivery drivers punch in a code, grab, and go — no line, no crowding, no "is this mine?" Countertop, floor, built-in, and outdoor configurations, installed and supported locally in Houston.

Series F2G

Flav-R 2-Go® Locker Systems

The workhorse of the lineup. Every locker has its own heated base, its own timer, and its own magnetic lock. Preset to 150°F, adjustable to 180°F, or switch heat off per-locker for ambient orders. A 10" touchscreen on one or both sides runs the whole thing — staff load from the back, guests enter a code on the front, and the right door lights up and opens.
