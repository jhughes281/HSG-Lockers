Why Smart Lockers

Your packages shouldn't sit in plain sight

A doorstep is not a delivery system. A smart locker takes the package out of sight and behind a locked steel door the moment the courier closes it — and it stays there until the recipient's own code opens it.

On the doorstep

Visible to anyone who walks or drives by
Anyone can pick it up — no questions asked
Exposed to rain, heat, and Houston humidity
A "delivered" photo is the only record of what happened

In a Zen Locker

Out of sight, behind a locked steel door
Opens only with the recipient's QR code or PIN
Indoor or weather-rated outdoor placement
Every drop-off and pickup logged with time and ID

From the Founder

I deliver packages for a living. I built Zen Lockers to end what I see at the curb every day.

Our founder drives for a national carrier. He has watched packages disappear minutes after the delivery photo was taken, and been met at the curb by strangers asking for parcels that weren't theirs. Zen Lockers exists to close that gap: from the driver's hand into locked steel, and only the right code opens the door. That is the whole company — nothing about it is theoretical.

Jay Hughes Founder, Zen Lockers · Houston

How Buying Works

From quote to first delivery

No sales funnel, no call center. You talk to the person who will be on site for the install.

STEP 01
Request a quote
Tell us the property type, unit count or daily order volume, and where the lockers would live. We reply within one business day.
STEP 02
Site walk & layout
We visit, measure, check power and network, and recommend a door mix and placement. You get a drawing and a fixed price.
STEP 03
Install & setup
Hardware is delivered and installed, software configured, and staff and residents onboarded — we stay until the first packages go in.
STEP 04
Local support
Service and support from Houston, not a ticket queue. If a door sticks or a screen goes dark, you call us and we come out.

Typical lead time: confirmed at quote based on configuration and freight. Pricing: hardware, installation, and software are itemized — no bundled mystery numbers.
