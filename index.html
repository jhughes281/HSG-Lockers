How buying works

From quote to first delivery.

No sales funnel, no call center. You talk to the person who will be on site for the install.

01 / QUOTE

Request a quote

Tell us the property type, unit count or daily order volume, and where the lockers would live. We reply within one business day.

02 / SITE WALK

Measure & plan

We visit, measure, check power and network, and recommend a door mix and placement. You get a drawing and a fixed price.

03 / INSTALL

Install & onboard

Hardware installed, software configured, staff and residents onboarded — we stay until the first packages go in.

04 / SUPPORT

Local support

Service from Houston, not a ticket queue. If a door sticks or a screen goes dark, you call the people who installed it.

Pricing: hardware, installation, and software are itemized — no bundled mystery numbers. Lead time: confirmed at quote based on configuration and freight.

All of them. The parcel lockers are carrier-agnostic — FedEx, UPS, Amazon, USPS, and food couriers all deliver into the same bank. The recipient gets a one-time code or QR the moment their delivery is placed.

Lockers have size limits, so we plan for it: the door mix we recommend at the site walk includes oversize doors sized to your delivery volume, and anything that still won't fit follows the drop-off policy you choose — door, office, or desk.

A standard 120V outlet and a network connection — ethernet, Wi-Fi, or cellular all work. We verify power, network, and clearances during the free site walk before you commit to anything.

We do — from Houston. There's no national call center in the middle: if a door sticks or a screen goes dark, you call the same people who did the install and we come out.

Lockers are the main event. For properties we already serve, we also place and stock vending machines and run a scheduled valet trash service — one local team for all three.
