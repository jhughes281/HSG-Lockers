Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 9, 2026

Zen Lockers ("we," "us") operates zenlockers.com. This policy explains what information we collect through this website and how we use it.

Information you provide

When you submit our quote request form or contact us by email or phone, we collect the information you choose to share — typically your name, email address, phone number, property type, and message. We use it solely to respond to your inquiry, prepare quotes, and provide our services.

Information collected automatically

This site does not set cookies and does not run advertising or cross-site tracking. Fonts are served by Google Fonts, which receives standard web request data (such as your IP address) when the page loads. If we enable website analytics in the future, we will use a cookieless, privacy-focused service that does not track you across other sites.

Form processing

Quote form submissions are delivered to our business inbox. If form delivery is handled by a third-party form service, that service processes your submission only to deliver it to us.

How we share information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only as needed to deliver our services (for example, with a manufacturer to prepare a quote for your property), to comply with law, or with your consent.

Data retention

We keep inquiry and quote information for as long as needed to serve you and maintain business records, then delete it.

Your choices

You may request a copy of the information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by contacting us at the email below.
