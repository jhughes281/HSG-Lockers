New · Mobile Event Lockers

Secure your items. Enjoy the event.

A branded shipping container full of smart lockers that arrives before gates open, runs self-service all event long, and leaves with the load-out. Guests store bags, purses, phones, and jackets behind a locked door instead of carrying them through the crowd.

The unit

Built to travel

Inside

High-capacity lockers

Walls of lockers in mixed sizes, a central touchscreen kiosk, and interior lighting for night events.

Outside

Branded & weather-ready

A steel container that is easy to spot from across the grounds. It can carry Zen Lockers, event, or sponsor branding.

On the move

Delivered & picked up

Arrives on a truck, sets up on site, and leaves after load-out, with no permanent installation at your venue.

How it works

Rent, lock, enjoy

1 · Rent at the kiosk

Guests pick a locker size on the touchscreen and pay by card or tap. No app download is needed.

2 · Lock it

The door opens, the guest stores their items, and they get a code to come back with.

3 · Enjoy the event

Guests return with the code to grab their items, and the locker is ready for the next person.

Common Questions

Is this available now?+

We're taking inquiries from event organizers and venues now. Tell us your event date, expected attendance, and location, and we'll follow up with availability and options.

Who pays: the guest or the event?+

Either. Lockers can be guest-paid at the kiosk, sponsored, or included in a ticket tier. We'll walk through the economics with you.

How much space does it need?+

Roughly a container-sized footprint plus room for a guest line and truck access. We confirm the exact layout on a site visit or from your site map.

Can it carry our branding or a sponsor's?+

Yes. The exterior and the kiosk screen can be branded for your event or a sponsor, and we confirm the artwork and timing in advance.
