Outdoor Parcel Lockers

The alfaBOX modular parcel locker system from ALFA 3, designed and built in the Czech Republic, for apartment communities, campuses, and commercial properties in Houston. Couriers drop packages behind a locked door, and only the recipient can open it. Zen Lockers handles the site walk, the quote, installation, and local service.

Three ways to power it

Pick the model that fits the site

Mains powered

alfaBOX Prime

Built for high-traffic sites. 10" or 15" sunlight-readable touchscreen, QR scanner, optional NFC/RFID reader, and options for a payment terminal, cameras, lighting, and a label printer.

Solar powered

alfaBOX Flex

Runs on its own solar power, so it can go where trenching a power line doesn't make sense: parking lots, perimeter walks, and remote buildings.

Battery powered

alfaBOX Lite

Battery-powered with no screen. Couriers and residents open doors from a mobile app, which makes it the lightest option to place and expand.

How it works on site

Delivered. Locked. Notified.

The courier scans in and drops the package into the right-size door. The door locks, and the resident gets a pickup code. Every open is logged with a time stamp. The leasing office stops being a package room.

Common Questions

How many doors does my property need?+

It depends on unit count, delivery volume, and how fast residents collect. On the site walk we look at your package volume and recommend a door mix. Because the system is modular, you can add columns later instead of overbuying on day one.

Which model is right for us?+

Prime fits high-traffic spots where power is available. Flex (solar) and Lite (battery) fit locations where running power is costly. We recommend one after the site walk.

Who installs it, and what do I need on site?+

Zen Lockers coordinates delivery, placement, anchoring, and setup. Your quote lists exactly what the site needs: a level surface, clearances, and power and network where the model requires them.

What does it cost?+

Every quote is itemized: hardware, installation, software or subscription fees, and the service plan. Request a quote and we reply within one business day to schedule a free site walk.
