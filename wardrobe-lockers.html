Before You Buy

What we confirm in writing

Every Zen Lockers quote is itemized. These are the questions we answer for your site before you sign anything.

Site requirements

Placement, floor or pad, anchoring, clearances, accessible reach range, and whether the location is indoor, covered, or fully exposed.

Power & network

U.S. power configuration (120 V) for the exact model, and whether the unit connects by wired LAN or cellular.

Software & ongoing costs

Hardware, installation, and any software or subscription fees are listed as separate lines, so you see the monthly cost before you commit.

Warranty & service

Manufacturer warranty terms, who handles each repair, and the local service plan you choose (Gold, Silver, or Bronze), all spelled out in the quote. See service plans →

Lead time & stages

Site walk, quote, order, delivery, install, configuration, and onboarding, with an estimated date for each step.

Certifications

Certifications belong to the manufacturer and the specific product line. We confirm which listings apply to the unit you order before you buy.

Common Questions

Can employees use their existing badges?+

Chip-reader access is supported, and larger projects can integrate with your corporate system. We confirm compatibility with your badge technology before you order.

Can lockers be shared instead of assigned?+

Yes. Lockers and access rights are managed in a web interface, so you can assign them permanently or hand them out per shift or per day.

What does it cost?+

Every quote is itemized: hardware, installation, software, and the service plan. Request a quote and we reply within one business day.
