Smart Charging Lockers

Phones, tablets, scanners, and radios charge behind a locked door instead of piling up on a break-room counter. One bay per user, power cut automatically at full charge, and a log of every opening — built for factories, warehouses, schools, and testing centers.

Parcel Lockers Food & Restaurant Asset Lockers Charging Lockers
Charging · 42-Bay Cabinet
  • USB or Type-C charging in every bay
  • One-to-one matching between users and devices
  • Automatic power cutoff when a device is fully charged
  • Overcharge, short-circuit, and overheat protection
  • Temperature sensor and fire suppression in each bay, plus a cabinet smoke sensor with audible & visual alarm
  • Face recognition, RFID card, or PIN access on a 10" touchscreen
  • 2.0mm steel shell, 304 stainless lining, explosion-proof aluminum-alloy doors
  • Cloud monitoring, off-peak charging control, and a full audit log
